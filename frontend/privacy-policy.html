Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Introduction

EasyLone (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a loan marketplace platform. We help users compare and apply for loans from multiple banks and NBFCs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our platform:

  • Name, mobile number, email address
  • Date of birth, city, and pincode
  • Employment type and income details
  • Loan amount and purpose
  • Credit-related information (through soft checks with your consent)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Show you personalized loan offers
  • Share your application with partner lenders (only with your permission)
  • Communicate with you about your applications
  • Improve our services

4. Soft Credit Check

When you check eligibility or submit an application, we may perform a soft credit check. A soft check does not affect your CIBIL score. A hard credit check is done only by the lender you finally choose.

5. Sharing of Information

We share your information only with:

  • Partner banks and NBFCs (when you choose to apply)
  • Service providers who help us operate the platform
  • Authorities if required by law

We never sell your personal data.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information using encryption and secure systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us. You can also withdraw consent for soft credit checks at any time.
